Dota 2 Carry Guide

🔥 Meta Carries

Phantom Assassin

Late Game Carry

Focus on farming fast and hitting strong late-game spikes.

Early: Battle Fury Mid: Desolator, BKB Late: Satanic, Abyssal Blade

Juggernaut

Balanced Carry

Strong in all phases with healing and powerful ultimate.

Early: Phase Boots, Maelstrom Mid: Manta Style, Aghanim Late: Butterfly, Swift Blink

Faceless Void

Teamfight Carry

Use Chronosphere wisely to control fights and win games.

Early: Power Treads, Mask of Madness Mid: Maelstrom, BKB Late: Mjollnir, Satanic

Terrorblade

Hyper Carry

Extremely strong late game with illusions and map pressure.

Early: Power Treads, Dragon Lance Mid: Manta Style, Skadi Late: Butterfly, Satanic
